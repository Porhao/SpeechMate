# SpeechMate

An AI speaking coach for Malaysian speakers (English, Bahasa Malaysia and Manglish), built as a Final Year
Project. Three functions: Conversation, Mock interview, Presentation practice (+ Q&A). Each session is
recorded, analysed after you finish (speech, voice, face and posture), and turned into four scores with
evidence-based coaching.
