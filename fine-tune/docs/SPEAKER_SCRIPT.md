# SpeechMate Recording Session: Practice Script

About 25 minutes, 36 short recordings. Print one copy for you (the host) and one for the speaker.

## Read this to the speaker first

> Thanks for helping! I'm recording how people in Malaysia really speak, to make SpeechMate understand Malaysian English better.
>
> - There are no right or wrong answers. Just talk like you normally would with friends.
> - Mixing English and Malay is good. So are "um", "lah", "kan" and so on. Please don't try to sound "proper".
> - Please **don't say** your full name, IC number, phone number or home address.
> - Aim for **10 to 30 seconds** per answer. I'll stop you if it goes long.
> - You can skip any question, and you can stop at any time.

## How to record (host)

1. Use the phone's voice recorder (or a laptop). Hold the phone about 30 cm from the speaker's mouth.
2. **Start a new recording for every number below**, so file 001 is answer 1, 002 is answer 2, and so on. If you skip a question, write "skipped" in the notes column and leave the number unused.
3. Ask the question in the style shown: casual Manglish for A, interviewer English for B, Malay for D. **Your style sets theirs.**
4. If an answer runs past 30 seconds, stop and start a new recording at the next pause (like C1 below).

## A. Warm-up chat (ask casually, in Manglish)

| File | Ask |
|---|---|
| 001 | What did you do last weekend ah? |
| 002 | Where's your favourite place to makan? Why that place? |
| 003 | Tell me about a time you got lost or something went wrong on a trip. |
| 004 | How do you usually get to uni or work? Got jam or not? |
| 005 | What's one thing you're stressed about this week? |
| 006 | Recommend me a show, movie or game, and convince me lah. |

## B. Mock interview (ask like an interviewer, in English)

| File | Ask |
|---|---|
| 009 | Tell me about yourself. |
| 010 | Why are you interested in this role, or the field you're studying? |
| 011 | What's your biggest strength? Give me an example. |
| 012 | Tell me about a weakness and what you're doing about it. |
| 013 | Describe a time you worked in a team and there was a conflict. |
| 014 | Tell me about a problem you solved at work, uni or in a project. |
| 015 | Where do you see yourself in five years? |
| 016 | Do you have any questions for us? (Any made-up question is fine.) |

## C. Presentation and Q&A

| File | Ask |
|---|---|
| 017–019 | Give a one-minute introduction to your final-year project, your job, or something you know well, as if presenting to a class. **Stop and restart the recording at a pause about every 20 seconds** (017, 018, 019). |
| 020 | Explain how to cook a dish you know, step by step. |
| 021 | "Why did you choose that approach?" (a follow-up on their talk) |
| 022 | "What would you do differently next time?" |

## D. Malay-leaning (ask in Malay)

## E. Numbers and names

| File | Ask |
|---|---|
| 028 | What did you spend money on yesterday? Roughly how much for each thing? |
| 029 | When is your birthday, and what's the plan for your next one? (Day and month only, not the year.) |
| 030 | What are your class or work hours? Walk me through a typical day with times. |
| 031 | Name a few companies, apps or brands you use every day, and what for. |

## F. Noisy repeats (ask any earlier question they liked again)

| File | Condition |
|---|---|
| 032 | Play a fan, TV or café-noise video in the background at normal volume. |
| 033 | Hold the phone at arm's length, or put the laptop about 1 metre away. |
| 034 | Outdoors, or next to a window with traffic. |

## G. Silence (no talking)

| File | What |
|---|---|
| 035 | 5–8 seconds of the speaker sitting quietly. |
| 036 | 5–8 seconds of only the background noise from 032. |
