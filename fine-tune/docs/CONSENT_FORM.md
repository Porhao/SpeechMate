## Part A. Participant information sheet (participant keeps this)

**Study title:** Evaluating SpeechMate, an AI speaking coach for Malaysian learners
**Researcher:** [Your name], [programme], [university] · [university email]
**Supervisor:** [Supervisor name] · [email]
**Ethics approval reference:** [number, once approved]

**What is this study about?**
SpeechMate gives feedback on spoken English: pronunciation, fillers ("um"), pace, confidence and body language. This study checks whether its measurements agree with what people actually said and with how human raters judge the same recordings.

**What will I be asked to do?**
Depending on the part you agree to below:
1. **Recording (about 10 minutes):** answer 9 short everyday questions in English, Malay or a mix, recorded on a phone. Optionally, a 1–2 minute video of you introducing yourself.
2. **Using the app (about 30 minutes):** practise with SpeechMate, then answer a short questionnaire about the feedback you received.

**Do I have to take part?**
No. Taking part is voluntary. You can skip any question, stop at any time, and withdraw your recordings until **[date, e.g. when analysis begins]** without giving a reason. Not taking part, or withdrawing, will not affect your grades, your job or your relationship with the university.

**What happens to my recordings?**
- You are identified only by a code (for example `s07`). Your name and the code are linked only on the signed consent form, which is stored separately from the recordings.
- You are asked not to say your full name, IC number, phone number or address. If you do, that part is cut or the clip is deleted.
- The researcher writes down exactly what you said (a transcript).
- The recordings are run through SpeechMate. This sends the **audio** to Groq (speech-to-text, servers in the United States) and the **text** of what you said to Google Gemini (servers outside Malaysia). Neither service receives your name. Video is analysed on the researcher's own server and is not sent to these services.
- **[2–3] trained raters** (students or staff, under the supervisor's direction) will listen to the audio and, if you agree, watch the video, to score confidence and pronunciation. They do not receive your name.
- Recordings are stored on [the researcher's password-protected laptop / university storage] and are never put on GitHub or published.
- **Retention:** recordings and transcripts are deleted by **[date, e.g. 6 months after the FYP is graded]**. Anonymised numbers (scores, error rates) may be kept and published.

**Will I be identifiable in the report?**
No. The report and any publication show only group results and, at most, short anonymised quotes from transcripts. No audio, video or images of you will be published.

**Are there risks or benefits?**
Risks are minimal; some people feel a little self-conscious being recorded. You may find the practice and feedback useful. [Describe any token of appreciation, or "There is no payment."]

**Your rights under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Malaysia):** you may ask to see, correct or delete your data by contacting the researcher.

**Questions or complaints:** the researcher or supervisor above, or [the university ethics committee contact].
